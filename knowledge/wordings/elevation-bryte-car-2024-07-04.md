# Wording Key Points: Elevation Technical Underwriters / Bryte, Contractors All Risk

- **Underwriting manager:** Elevation Technical Underwriters (Pty) Ltd, FSP 54974
- **Insurer:** Bryte Insurance Company Limited, FSP 17703
- **Wording file:** `Elevation_Technical_Underwriters-CAR-04-07-2024_Bryte`
- **Applies to:** schedules with policy numbers starting `ETU`

The agent should read the full wording when it's available. This file is the fallback and the quick reference.

## General exceptions (Sections 1 and 2)
1. Riot, strike, civil commotion, war, terrorism, political acts. *Covered separately by SASRIA in SA.*
2. Nuclear and radioactive contamination.
3. Disease or impairment from a gradual cause.
4. The deductibles stated in the schedule.
5. Asbestos, in any form.
6. Sanctions (UN, EU, UK, US).
7. Computer and date-related losses.
8. Communicable disease.
9. Cyber and malicious use of computer systems.
10. **Wilful act, omission or gross negligence of any director, officer, manager or responsible site official.**

## General conditions that matter at claim stage
- **Claims notice:** written notice (email) to insurers **within 30 days** of the event. Report theft or wilful damage to police.
- **Rejected claims:** the client must start legal action within **12 months** of a written rejection, or the claim is treated as abandoned.
- **Prescription:** no liability for loss after 12 months unless the claim is in court or arbitration.
- **Premium:** due in advance. If a debit order fails, it's re-debited the next month. If it fails again, the policy is cancelled from the date of the first missed payment.
- **Material change in risk:** must be reported immediately.
- **Premium adjustment:** a final contract value or turnover declaration is required.
- **No admission of liability** without the insurer's consent.
- **Cancellation:** 30 days' notice. On annual policies, run-off continues for contracts in progress.
- **Deductibles:** where more than one could apply, only the **largest** applies.

## Contract Works exclusions
1. Losses found only through inventory or stocktaking.
2. Consequential loss.
3. Cost of fixing **defective material, workmanship, design or specification**. Resulting damage is covered, the defect itself is not. Betterment is not covered.
4. Marine or air transit outside the territorial limits.
5. **Work stopped for more than 90 consecutive days** on a site.
6. Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, normal upkeep, continuous dewatering.

Mechanical and electrical plant contracts also exclude: breakdown of used plant, breakdown of new plant after testing, refractory linings, and failure to follow procedures or overriding safety devices.

**Basis of settlement:** property under 3 years old is covered for repair or replacement cost. Property over 3 years old is capped at market value (20-year depreciation, 20% residual).

## Public Liability exclusions
1. Injury to the insured's own employees (COIDA).
2. Motor vehicles on public roads, aircraft and watercraft, with exceptions for vehicles used as tools, loading and unloading, and vehicles parked on site.
3. Compulsory motor liability.
4. Damage to the insured's own property, or property they are working on caused by their own defective work. **Intentional removal of lateral support** is excluded, but liability from shock, vibration or negligence is not.
5. Damage to property insured under the Contract Works section.
6. Contractual penalties and liquidated damages beyond common-law liability.
7. Making good faulty workmanship.

## General memorandum
- Pollution, unless sudden and accidental and it happens during the period.
- Cash and securities.
- **Liquidated damages, penalties for delay, and performance or efficiency guarantees.**
- Gradual-cause disease.

## Who is insured
Who is insured depends on whether the policy is principal-controlled or contractor-arranged, annual or specific. Subcontractors, the employer and consultants are insured "to the extent required by contract". **Joint insured and cross liability on the schedule still matter.** If the schedule says "No", flag it for any principal contract that requires them.

## Specific contract policies
If the contract isn't finished by the end of the period, cover continues **on terms to be agreed**. This is **not automatic**, so an extension must be requested before expiry.
