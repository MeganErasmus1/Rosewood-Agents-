# POPIA: Clause for AI-Assisted Processing

Have your compliance officer review this before use. It's a draft, not legal advice.

## Why you need it
- **Section 18:** clients must be told how their information is processed and by whom.
- **Section 21:** every service provider that touches client data (Anthropic for Claude, Make, Microsoft) is an **operator** and needs a written agreement. Each provider's commercial terms and data processing agreement cover this. Download them and keep copies on file.
- **Section 71:** no decision may be made about a client solely by automated means. The clause confirms that a person reviews everything.
- **Section 72:** Anthropic and Make process data outside South Africa. That needs adequate protection, client consent, or both. The clause does both.

---

## A. Clause for the Broker Appointment / Client Mandate

> **Use of technology and artificial intelligence**
>
> To service your insurance efficiently, Rosewood Insurance Brokers (Pty) Ltd uses secure software, including artificial intelligence (AI) tools, to read, summarise and organise your policy documents and to manage renewals. These tools process the information in your policy documents, such as company and contact details, contract details, cover and premium information, only to service your insurance.
>
> - Everything an AI tool produces is reviewed by a Rosewood representative before it is sent to you or relied on. AI tools do not make decisions about you. All advice is given by Rosewood's authorised representatives.
> - Our service providers act as operators on our behalf under written terms that require them to keep your information confidential and secure, and not to use it for their own purposes, including training AI models.
> - Some of these providers store or process information outside South Africa. We only use providers that are bound by laws, agreements or binding corporate rules that give protection substantially similar to the Protection of Personal Information Act.
> - By signing this mandate, you consent to this processing, including the transfer of information outside South Africa for this purpose. You may withdraw consent or object at any time by contacting our Information Officer at {email}. If you do, we will continue to service your policies without these tools.

## B. Line for the Privacy Notice / PAIA Manual

> Rosewood uses AI-assisted software, provided by operators under written agreements, to summarise policy documents and manage renewals. Outputs are reviewed by a person before use. Some processing takes place outside South Africa, subject to adequate safeguards in terms of section 72 of POPIA.
