# Template: Policy Record (client file)

Saved as `{YYYY-MM-DD} {Client} {Policy No} Policy Record.pdf` in the client's folder.

This record has two parts. **Part A** is filled in by the agent. **Part B** is the advice record and must be completed by the broker. The agent never fills Part B, because a FAIS record of advice must reflect the broker's actual needs analysis and reasons, not a summary of the policy.
